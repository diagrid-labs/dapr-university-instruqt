The sandbox for this challenge is being prepared, it should be ready within a few seconds. Once it's ready, click the *Start* button.

In this challenge you'll find out what each field of a secret store component does, read secrets one at a time and in bulk, and see a state store component get its password from the secret store with `secretKeyRef`.

If you need more information about secret store components during (or after) the challenge, visit the [Dapr Docs](https://docs.dapr.io/reference/components-reference/supported-secret-stores/file-secret-store/).
