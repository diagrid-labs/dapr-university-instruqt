In this lab you'll find out what each field of a secret store component does, read secrets one at a time and in bulk, and see a state store component get its password from the secret store with `secretKeyRef`.

If you need more information about secret store components during (or after) the lab, visit the [Dapr Docs](https://docs.dapr.io/reference/components-reference/supported-secret-stores/file-secret-store/).
