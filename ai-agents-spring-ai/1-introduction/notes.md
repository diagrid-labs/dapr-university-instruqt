Click the *Start* button to setup the sandbox environment for this training, this may take up to 2 minutes. Once the environment is ready, click the *Start* button again.

- There are 4 challenges to complete, each takes about 5-9 minutes. If your session is idle for more than 10 minutes, the session will stop and you'll need to restart the track.
- You can extend the time with 10 minutes if you run out of time.
- Tracks can be restarted up to 5 times.

---

Spring AI gives you the building blocks for an agent. In this first challenge you'll learn what makes it durable, why that matters for a tool with a real side effect, and get your Catalyst account and sandbox ready. This challenge takes about 9 minutes.

### What you'll learn in this challenge

- What `diagrid-spring-ai-starter` adds to a Spring AI `ChatClient`
- Why idempotency matters once a tool has a real side effect
- How to create a Diagrid Catalyst account and log in from the CLI
- Where to add your OpenAI API key
