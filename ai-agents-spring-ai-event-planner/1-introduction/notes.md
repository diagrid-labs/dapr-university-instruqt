Click the *Start* button to setup the sandbox environment for this training, this may take up to 2 minutes. Once the environment is ready, click the *Start* button again.

- There are 4 challenges to complete, each takes about 6-8 minutes. If your session is idle for more than 10 minutes, the session will stop and you'll need to restart the track.
- You can extend the time with 10 minutes if you run out of time.
- Tracks can be restarted up to 5 times.

---

Spring AI gives you the building blocks for an agent. In this first challenge you'll learn what makes it durable, why idempotent tools matter, and get your Catalyst account and sandbox ready. This challenge takes about 8 minutes.

### What you'll learn in this challenge

- What `diagrid-spring-ai-starter` adds to a Spring AI `ChatClient`
- Why a durable tool call needs to be safe to run more than once
- How to create a Diagrid Catalyst account and log in from the CLI
